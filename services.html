Full Scope

What we offer, in detail

Due to its sensitive nature, motor vehicle loss assessment is focused on providing underwriters with prompt, optimum and competitive professional services — not only for customers but also interested parties.

Motor Vehicle Loss Assessment 01

Our team of qualified professionals personally visit and thoroughly inspect accident vehicles, source parts from different reputable dealers, and compile a comprehensive, reliable report in relation to the provided documentation regarding the loss.

Motor Vehicle Valuation 02

Valuation is done for collateral in support of a loan application, pre-insurance, pre/post accident, court bails and bonds, buying and selling, inventories, and any other reason the principal may request. To achieve this, we establish the physical existence of the subject vehicle or machine, professionally gauge the prevailing market value, and verify legal registration particulars. The vehicle is thoroughly checked to establish its coach work and mechanical condition before testing its road-worthiness, credit-worthiness and insurability. A report is compiled showing our professional view of market value, defects noted (if any), and recommendations.

Technical / Mechanical Inspection 03

We are equipped with qualified professionals who have vast experience in technical vehicle inspection to establish the condition of various vehicle systems and their serviceability. Mechanical inspection is carried out to establish the overall condition of a vehicle or machinery, both for insurance companies and financial institutions. A vehicle or machine repossessed due to failure of performance by the borrower is inspected not only to establish current value and cost of rehabilitation, but also its force-sale value if it is to be sold by auction.

Consultation 04

For companies wishing to upgrade their fleet of vehicles or sell existing ones, we undertake a market survey and testing of vehicles or machines on their behalf, and advise on the best value suitable for a given purpose. We are also consulted on various issues pertaining to motor assessment and valuation.

Turnaround Time 05

While this is a subject issue due to the varied nature of loss assessment and valuation, our normal turnaround time is invariably 16 working hours from the time of appointment. Should there be a need for a longer duration, we notify the institution in writing of the reason for the delay and continually update them on progress.
