Who We Are

Independent motor assessors built on integrity, honesty & professionalism

Mema Auto Assessors Limited was founded by two seasoned professionals, Maurice Juma and Bernard Meso. Our foundation rests on deep experience in motor vehicle assessment, motor valuation, repair costing, and pre- and post-accident vehicle inspection and valuation.

Our in-house assessors bring a broad spectrum of pooled experience and qualifications, enabling them to handle any assessment with efficiency — drawing on each other's knowledge to reach the best possible decision.

  • Authorized and regulated by the Insurance Regulatory Authority (IRA/13/278)
  • Registered members of the Motor Assessors Association of Kenya (MAAK)
  • Registered with KRA for income tax and VAT compliance

What We Do

Scope of Professional Services

From loss assessment to technical inspection, we provide underwriters, financiers and private clients with prompt, optimum and competitive professional services.

01

Motor Vehicle Loss Assessment

Our qualified professionals personally visit and thoroughly inspect accident vehicles, sourcing parts from reputable dealers before compiling comprehensive, reliable reports.

02

Motor Vehicle Valuation

Valuations for collateral, loan applications, pre-insurance, pre/post-accident, court bails and bonds, buying and selling, and inventories.

03

Technical / Mechanical Inspection

In-depth technical inspection to establish the condition and serviceability of vehicle systems — for insurers, financiers and repossessed assets alike.

04

Consultation

Market surveys and vehicle testing for fleet upgrades or disposal, with expert advice on the best value suited to your purpose.
